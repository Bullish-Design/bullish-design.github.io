Bullish Design provides product design consulting for mechanical and industrial products.

Good design decisions connect the job a product must do with the mechanisms, materials, and production choices that shape it. This site looks at those connections through project work and sourced design studies.

Field Notes covers notable modern and historical products. Each study separates documented facts from design interpretation and links to its sources.
